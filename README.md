# Hi, I'm Léo Milecki 👋

I'm a **Postdoctoral Associate at Weill Cornell Medicine**, working with **Mert Sabuncu** and **Qingyu Zhao** in the Machine Intelligence in NeuroImaging ([MINI](https://mini-cornell.github.io/)) lab.

My research focuses on developing **machine learning methods for biomedical data**, particularly representation learning, self-supervised learning, and multimodal and longitudinal modeling.

I'm interested in developing ML methods that go beyond task-specific predictions to better model biological systems and individual variability, with applications in **neuroimaging, medical imaging, and clinical data**.

## Research Projects

### Postdoctoral Research | Weill Cornell Medicine (2024–present)

**Continuous representations of brain function** *(Present)*

Exploring implicit neural representations to model fMRI signals and functional connectivity continuously, beyond fixed spatial parcellations and discrete descriptions of brain dynamics.

**Longitudinal modeling of multimorbidity trajectories** *(Present)*

Developed transformer-based models of longitudinal electronic health records to forecast comorbidities and investigate patient subtypes and trajectories across substance use, mental health, and chronic physical conditions using All of Us Research Data.

**Self-supervised representation learning for functional connectivity** *(MICCAI 2026)*

Developed a transformer-based framework with network-aware bilinear tokenization and masked autoencoding to learn representations of resting-state functional connectivity.

*Network-Aware Bilinear Tokenization for Brain Functional Connectivity Representation Learning.* [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14048) · [Code](https://github.com/leomlck/NERVE)

**Individual differences in brain function and behavior** *(2024-Present)*

Studied associations between resting-state functional connectivity, behavior, cognition, and psychiatric symptoms across large developmental neuroimaging cohorts.

- *Sex-Specific Associations Between Functional Brain Connectivity and Psychopathology* — Translational Psychiatry, 2025. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41398-025-03678-9) · [Code](https://github.com/leomlck/rCCA_ABCD_rsfMRI_CBCL)
- *Socioemotional and Executive Control Mismatch in Adolescence and Risks for Initiating Drinking* — JAMA Network Open, 2025. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.31378)
- *Heterogeneous Brain Functional Mapping of Childhood Psychiatric Symptoms* — Society for Neuroscience, 2024. [Abstract](https://www.abstractsonline.com/pp8/#!/20433/presentation/30189) · Selected for Neuroscience Press Conference (top 0.5% of 10,500 abstracts).

### PhD Research | CentraleSupélec / Université Paris-Saclay (2020–2024)

[My PhD](https://theses.hal.science/tel-04702855) focused on **longitudinal 3D MRI for renal transplantation monitoring**, combining self-supervised representation learning, multimodal modeling, and clinical prediction.

**Radiomics and renal graft survival prediction** *(Academic Radiology, 2025)*

Investigated MRI-derived representations and radiomics for predicting long-term renal graft survival.

*Renal transplant survival prediction from unsupervised deep learning-based radiomics on early dynamic contrast-enhanced MRI.* [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.acra.2025.05.001)

**Multimodal MRI representation learning with clinical information** *(MIDL 2024)*

Developed a CLIP-inspired framework using LLM-generated clinical descriptions to align 3D MRI representations with multiple clinical variables and improve renal function forecasting.

*MEDIMP: Medical Images and Clinical Prompts for Multimodal Representation Learning.* [Paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v227/milecki24a.html) · [Code](https://github.com/leomlck/MEDIMP)

**Contrastive learning and longitudinal MRI modeling** *(MICCAI 2022)*

Developed clinically guided contrastive pretraining of 3D MRI representations, followed by masked transformer modeling to forecast renal function over time.

*Contrastive Masked Transformers for Longitudinal 3D Medical Images.* [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-16449-1_24) · [Code](https://github.com/leomlck/renal_transplant_imaging)

**Unsupervised kidney segmentation** *(ISBI 2021)*

Developed unsupervised deep learning methods for kidney segmentation in MRI to support downstream longitudinal imaging analyses.

*3D unsupervised kidney graft segmentation based on deep learning and multi-sequence MRI.* [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISBI48211.2021.9433854)
